# Evaluator Context

Structural Assurability is evaluated under stated conditions rather than from
the system in isolation.

The evaluator model records conditions concerning knowledge, access, trust,
and cooperation.

Resource bounds separately represent practical constraints on evidence
acquisition.

As a result, the same system and claim may yield different obtainable evidence
under different evaluator conditions or resource bounds.

A statement that a claim is not resolvable under one evaluator context does not
imply that it is intrinsically unresolvable under every possible context.
