# Structural Assurability

Structural Assurability represents the claim-relative evidentiary capabilities
available for evaluating a system under specified evaluator and resource
conditions.

It is a **profile, not a universal scalar score**.

Two systems may expose different raw evidence while enabling the same
claim-material evidentiary capabilities.
They may therefore be equivalent for one assurance context.

Conversely, systems with similar functional behavior may support different
assurance-relevant distinctions and therefore have different Structural
Assurability profiles.

The relation is intentionally contextual rather than a universal ranking of
architectures.
