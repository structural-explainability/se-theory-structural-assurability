# Claims

Structural Assurability is **claim-relative**.

A claim is represented formally as a predicate over possible worlds.
The same system may therefore have different Structural Assurability
profiles for different claims.

This prevents architectural features from being treated as universally useful
for assurance.
Evidence that matters for one claim may contribute nothing to another.

The theory also distinguishes claim truth from claim resolvability.
A claim may have a definite truth value in the modeled world
even when the evaluator cannot resolve that value
from the obtainable observations.
