# Composition and Extension

The theorem layer studies how changes in evaluation conditions or system
capabilities propagate through the Structural Assurability model.

**Expanded evaluator access** can enlarge the set of obtainable evidence when
resources are held fixed.

**Expanded resources** can enlarge the set of obtainable evidence when access
conditions are held fixed.

Neither result alone implies strict improvement in Structural Assurability.
Additional evidence may be immaterial to the claim or may fail to enable a new
evidentiary capability.

Capability monotonicity is therefore represented as an explicit condition
rather than assumed universally.

A conservative extension establishes strict comparative assurability when the
extended system preserves the weaker system's capabilities and adds at least
one additional capability.
