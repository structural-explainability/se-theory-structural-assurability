# Evidence

The formalization distinguishes several questions about evidence.

**Evidence** may exist without being accessible to the evaluator.

**Accessible evidence** may still be impractical to obtain within the available
resources.

**Obtainable evidence** may still be irrelevant to the specified claim.

**Claim-material evidence** may enable different evidentiary capabilities depending
on the capability model.

Evidentiary sufficiency is represented separately from structural
obtainability.
The existence or quantity of evidence does not by itself prove
that an assurance claim is adequately supported.
